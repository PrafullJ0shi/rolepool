          3Feeds and opt-in alerts

          Nothing here needs an account, an email address or a paid service. Pick one of three ways to hear about new roles.

          3.1Atom feeds

          Each feed lists up to 200 roles, newest first by posting date (or first-seen date), and refreshes after every daily sweep. Paste a URL into any feed reader.

          FeedRoles nowURL

          3.2GitHub watch alerts

          The repository keeps one open issue per role family, titled Alerts: Engineering, Alerts: Marketing and so on. After each sweep the workflow posts the day’s new roles in that family as a comment, using the repository’s own token. Open the issue and press Subscribe; GitHub then emails or notifies you with each digest. Unsubscribe the same way.

          3.3Watch a filter in this browser

          On the Board, set any filters and press Watch this filter. The filter is kept in this browser only (local storage). On your next visit the board opens with that filter and a line saying how many matching roles are new since you last looked.

          4Method

          How each number on this site is made, and where it can be wrong. The same text, with the code paths, is in the repository README.

          4.1Sources

          Two kinds of source, both public and both read with a named User-Agent, at most two requests a second per host, retries with back-off on 429 and 5xx, and an ETag cache.

          1. Company boards. The official job-board APIs that applicant tracking systems publish for exactly this use: Greenhouse boards-api, Lever postings, Ashby posting-api, Workable widget, SmartRecruiters postings and Recruitee offers. The list of boards is in data/companies.yml: seeded from the public company list of crypto-jobs-fyi/crawler and extended by probing a few hundred more crypto firms, keeping only boards whose postings read as crypto.
          2. Job boards. Crypto job boards and remote boards that publish an RSS feed or API, used under their stated terms (link back, name the source, call limits). Items from general remote boards are kept only if they pass the crypto keyword test (relevance ≥ 2: a strong term such as blockchain, stablecoin or DeFi counts 1, a weak one such as wallet or token counts 0.5).

          Not used: LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, Wellfound and any site whose terms forbid automated access, plus any board that needs an API key.

          4.2One opening, one row

          url′ = https + lower(host) + path without trailing “/”, minus utm_*, gh_src, ref, source, lever-source, fbclid, gclid key = sha1( company‾ | title‾ | place )[0:16] company‾ = lower(company) − legal suffix (Inc, Ltd, GmbH, AG…) − TLD (.com, .io, .xyz…), letters and digits only title‾ = lower(title) − (bracketed text) − {remote, hybrid, full-time, contract…}; sr→senior, jr→junior, eng→engineer, &→and place = sorted country codes (max 3) | “remote” | “any”

          Exact key matches collapse into one row. A job-board item is merged into a company’s own posting when the company names match after also removing words such as Labs, Foundation or Network, and the titles are equal or have a token Jaccard similarity of at least 0.85. The company’s own posting wins; the board is listed under “also on”.

          4.3Detection

          salary
          Structured pay fields first (Ashby compensation, Lever salaryRange, Recruitee, Remote OK, Himalayas). Otherwise the first currency-marked range in the listed salary text or description, e.g. “$120,000 – $150,000”, “€80k–100k”, “CHF 120’000”, “12–18 LPA”. Period from nearby words (per hour, monthly, p.a.); annualised with hour×2080, day×260, week×52, month×12; converted to USD at the ECB rate; dropped outside 8k–1.5M USD.
          workplace
          The ATS field when present (Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee, Workable). Otherwise “hybrid”, “remote/anywhere/worldwide/distributed” or “on-site” in the title or location; a named place with no such word counts as on-site; nothing at all is “unknown”.
          country, region
          A fixed table of countries, US states and about 220 cities maps to ISO codes, then to North America, Latin America, Europe, Middle East & Africa or Asia-Pacific. Words such as EMEA, APAC, LATAM add regions. Remote with no place is “Global”.
          level
          Title rules in order: Intern, Executive (chief, VP, president, general counsel), Director (director, head of), Staff+ (staff, principal, architect), Manager (engineering manager, team lead, lead), Senior, Entry (junior, graduate, associate), Mid. No marker reads as Mid.
          role family
          Fifteen families from title keyword rules in a fixed order (security before engineering, design before product, sales engineer before engineer), then the department name, else Other.

          4.4Opened, closed, reposted

          opened key present now, absent from state (or closed in state) closed key open in state, absent now, AND its source fetched without error this sweep repost a key reopens within 90 days of closing → reposts + 1 opened_at = ATS posting date if sent, else first-seen time; unknown for roles already open at the first sweep of a board

          A board that fails to load never produces closes. Its roles stay open and the company is marked CHECK-BOARD until it loads again.

          4.5Hiring Momentum, H

          r_d = (O_d − C_d) / max(N_{d−1}, 5) daily net-open rate M = Σ_{a=0..29} w_a · r_{t−a} / Σ w_a, w_a = 2^(−a/7) 30-day decayed mean, 7-day half-life x = asinh(M / 0.01) tames the long right tail z_own = (x − mean(x_hist)) / (sd(x_hist) + 0.05) against the company’s own past z_xs = (x − median(x_all)) / max(1.4826 · MAD(x_all), 0.5) against all companies today s = n / (n + 14) n = days of own history z = s · z_own + (1 − s) · z_xs, clipped to [−6, 6] H = 100 · tanh(z / 3) −100 … +100

          Od counts roles whose opened_at falls on day d, which lets posting dates fill the first weeks before the sweep has its own history; Cd counts closes seen by the sweep. Early on H leans on the cross-section; after two weeks of daily sweeps, own history carries half the weight.

          4.6Freeze detector

          CHECK-BOARD board failed this sweep, or dropped to 0 open after > 0 (until 2 zero sweeps in a row) FROZEN board read fine and 0 roles open (after the CHECK-BOARD grace) or days since a new role ≥ 21 AND open ≤ 70% of its 30-day max or days since a new role ≥ 60 HIRING H ≥ 25 AND new roles in 7 days ≥ 2 COOLING H ≤ −25, or net 30-day opens < 0 AND days since a new role ≥ 10 STEADY everything else

          Rules are checked top to bottom. Every change of state is written to feed/flips.xml.

          4.7Freshness and ghost-job score

          age = now − opened_at (or first seen, when opened_at is unknown) F = 100 · e^(−age/30) G = 100 · (1 − Π (1 − p_i)) p_age = 0.6 · σ((age − 60) / 15) p_repost = min(0.5, 0.25 · reposts) p_evergreen = 0.5 if the title reads “general application”, “talent pool”, “future opportunities”… p_thin = 0.15 if the company posting has under 300 characters of description p_company = 0.3 if the company is FROZEN, 0.15 if COOLING bands: G < 25 low · 25–50 watch · ≥ 50 likely stale (hidden by “hide likely stale”)

          G is a heuristic, not a verdict. A long-open senior role can be real; a fresh evergreen post can be a talent pool.

          4.8Limits

          • First seen is not posted. Before the first sweep, only the employer’s own posting date is known.
          • Coverage is the company list plus five boards. Firms on custom careers sites or on Workday are not swept.
          • Mixed companies (a broker or payments firm with a crypto arm) are filtered to postings that read as crypto, which can miss a role that never names the field.
          • Remote OK, Remotive and Himalayas are general boards; their items pass a keyword test that can let a forex or fintech role through.
          • Rules are English-first; titles in other languages fall back to department names.
